What is Specora?

SPECORA(SPEC + ORA), is a word that originates from "specialisation" and "exploration". Specora is a short, interactive quiz built for incoming Software Engineering students. It asks about the activities and problems you enjoy, then suggests which specialisation might suit you best.

N.B: Your result is guidance only. It does not make your academic decision for you.

How it works

  1. 1

    Enter your details

    Tell us your name, student ID, and institutional email.

  2. 2

    Complete the quiz

    Answer 10 short questions about what you enjoy doing.

  3. 3

    Explore your result

    See your strongest matching specialisation and what's next.

The four specialisations

Full-Stack Web Development

Building complete websites and applications, front-end to back-end.

Machine Learning

Teaching computers to find patterns and make predictions from data.

AR/VR Development

Creating immersive augmented and virtual reality experiences.

Low-Level Programming

Working close to hardware: memory, performance, and systems.
